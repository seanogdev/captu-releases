Captu turns a corner of your screen into a clean video in a few seconds. Press the shortcut, drag
over what you want to show, and record. When you stop, trim the ends and send it on its way.

## Features

### ⚡ Starts in a keystroke

Press **⌘⇧2**, or click the menu bar icon, and drag over any part of your screen. Captu remembers
your last area, so recording the same spot again is one key away.

### 🎯 Records just what you pick

Capture a slice of your screen or a whole display. Your cursor, click highlights, system audio, and
microphone are each one switch away, at 30 or 60 frames per second.

### 🤫 Stays out of your way

There is no window to manage while you record, just the menu bar icon showing that a recording is
running.

### ✂️ Trim before you share

When you stop, the recording opens in a preview where you can trim the start and end, then save it,
copy it, or reveal it in Finder.

### 🖥️ Sharp on Retina

Recordings are captured at your display's full pixel resolution, in H.264 or HEVC.

### 🔄 Updates itself

Captu checks for new versions and shows you what changed before you install.

## Install

1. Download the `.dmg` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Captu** into **Applications**.
3. Launch it and grant **Screen Recording** when macOS asks. Microphone access is only requested
   if you turn the microphone on.

Every build is signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, so it opens without
any Gatekeeper workarounds.

## Requirements

- macOS 15 Sequoia or later
- A Mac with Apple Silicon
